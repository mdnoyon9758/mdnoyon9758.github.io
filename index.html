Private by design

The short version of our privacy policy.

  • Your scans stay on your phone. Photos, PDFs and the text read from them are stored only in the app. We run no servers that receive them.
  • No account needed. Open the app and scan. An optional PIN app lock keeps others out.
  • Text recognition runs on your device. Extra languages are downloaded once from the open-source Tesseract project; no document content is sent.
  • AI only when you choose. AI is off by default. If you turn it on with your own key, only that document goes straight from your phone to the provider you picked.
  • Ads from Google AdMob. Ads may use your advertising ID and approximate location. In Europe you are asked for consent first, and you can change it in Settings.
  • Pay once, no ads. ScanMate Pro is bought through Google Play; after that no ads are loaded. Delete the app and everything it stored is gone.
