Private by design

The short version of our privacy policy.

  • Your scans stay on your phone. Photos, PDFs and the text read from them are stored only in the app. We run no servers that receive them.
  • No account needed. Open the app and scan. An optional PIN app lock keeps others out, and a backup to one file keeps your documents safe.
  • Text recognition runs on your device. Extra languages are downloaded once from the open-source Tesseract project; no document content is sent.
  • AI only when you choose. AI is off by default. If you turn it on with your own key, only that document goes straight from your phone to the provider you picked.
  • Ads from Google AdMob. Ads may use your advertising ID and approximate location. In Europe you are asked for consent first, and you can change it in Settings.
  • Pay once, no ads. ScanMate Pro is bought through Google Play; after that no ads are loaded. Delete the app and everything it stored is gone.

Questions

 

Where do I get ScanMate AI?

It is free on Google Play, for Android 7.0 and newer.

Does it add a watermark?

No. Your PDFs are clean, in the free version too.

Does it read Bangla?

Yes. Add Bangla once in the text recognition languages and it works offline. Hindi, Arabic, Urdu and 40+ more are available too. Old PDFs typed in Bijoy fonts become real, searchable Bangla.

Can it make the photo and signature for a job application?

Yes. Tools › ID photo makes the job form photo (300 × 300 px, under 100 KB, white background) and the signature (300 × 80 px, under 60 KB) from a photo of your signature on paper, ready to upload.

Can it open PDFs from WhatsApp or Gmail?

Yes. Choose ScanMate AI when Android asks how to open a PDF, or open it from Tools › PDF reader. Password PDFs such as bank statements open too.

Do I need an AI subscription?

No. All scanning, PDF and text tools work without AI. AI tools are optional and use your own key; Google Gemini has a free tier.
